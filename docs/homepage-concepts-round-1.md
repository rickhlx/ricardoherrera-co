# Homepage concepts — round 1 (25)

Canvas: "Studio Homepage Concepts" (Design artifact). Placeholders throughout: "Your Name", [Project name], [Latest post title], [email]; imagery is CSS/SVG/canvas-generated.

## Vintage audio
1. Receiver Tuner Dial — sections are FM stations; turn the knob or press presets.
2. Two-Sided Mixtape — side A work, side B writing; working transport controls and J-card tracklist.
3. Meter Bridge — VU meters driven by the cursor; equipment-rack projects, patch-bay contact.
4. Record Crate — pull a project sleeve onto the turntable; tonearm drop/lift.
5. Mixing Desk — channel strips as nav; solo switches section, faders weight the mix.

## Photography & street
6. Contact Sheet Loupe — magnifying loupe over a grease-penciled contact sheet of projects.
7. Darkroom Develop — page develops up from blank paper under safelight; "lights on" toggle.
8. Rangefinder Focus — align the double image to bring your name into focus; frameline lenses.
9. Photobook Spreads — two-ink photobook with 3D page turns.
10. Night Flash — live canvas night street; shutter button fires a flash.

## 70s tech
11. Front Panel — toggle switches navigate and light binary address lamps.
12. Teletext Page 100 — dial page numbers on a remote keypad.

14. Exploded Manual — isometric exploded diagram of you; click parts to read sections.

## Midcentury modern

19. Record Bin Blog — jazz-sleeve posts flip forward; record slides out.
20. Textile Repeat — cursor-reactive pattern with colorways and scale.
